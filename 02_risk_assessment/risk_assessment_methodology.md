# Risk Assessment Methodology

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the methodology used to identify, assess, and prioritize risks within the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process.

The methodology is designed to support a consistent and risk-based approach to internal control assessment.

---

## 2. Risk Assessment Approach

Risks were evaluated based on two primary dimensions:

- Likelihood
- Impact

The overall risk rating is determined by considering the potential consequence of the risk and the likelihood that the risk could occur if the control environment is ineffective.

### Risk Model

**Risk Rating = Likelihood × Impact**

---

## 3. Likelihood Rating

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | The risk is unlikely to occur under normal circumstances. |
| Medium | The risk could occur periodically or under certain conditions. |
| High | The risk is likely to occur or has a significant opportunity to occur. |

---

## 4. Impact Rating

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| Low | Limited operational or financial impact. |
| Medium | Potentially significant operational, financial, or reporting impact. |
| High | Potential material financial loss, fraud exposure, regulatory concern, or significant operational disruption. |

---

## 5. Overall Risk Classification

| Risk Level | General Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Low | Risk can generally be managed through existing controls and routine monitoring. |
| Medium | Management attention and control improvement may be required. |
| High | Priority management attention and stronger preventive or detective controls are required. |

---

## 6. Risk Categories

The assessment considers the following risk categories:

### Financial Risk

Risk of financial loss, inaccurate payments, incorrect liabilities, or unreliable financial information.

### Operational Risk

Risk arising from ineffective or inefficient processes.

### Fraud Risk

Risk of intentional manipulation, unauthorized transactions, fictitious suppliers, or inappropriate use of company resources.

### Compliance Risk

Risk of failure to comply with internal policies, delegated authority, contractual requirements, or applicable regulations.

### Information / Access Risk

Risk arising from inappropriate system access, unauthorized changes, or inadequate segregation of duties.

---

## 7. Control Considerations

When assessing each risk, the following control characteristics are considered:

- Control objective
- Control owner
- Preventive or detective nature
- Frequency
- Evidence of performance
- Degree of automation
- Segregation of duties
- Management oversight
- Ability to detect or prevent the identified risk

---

## 8. Risk Prioritization

High-risk items receive priority during control testing and remediation planning.

Priority should be given to risks that could result in:

- Material financial loss
- Fraud
- Unauthorized payments
- Significant process disruption
- Material financial reporting errors
- Significant control failure
- Repeated or unresolved exceptions

---

## 9. Assessment Principle

The purpose of risk assessment is not to eliminate all risk.

The objective is to determine whether risks are:

**Identified → Assessed → Controlled → Monitored → Remediated**

where necessary.
